name: Party game rules
description: User decisions replacing the original ten-round crowd-frequency game
---
The live game has seven timed rounds using the supplied spreadsheet's prepared questions and answer rarity groups, not the frequency of guests' answers.

**Why:** The user requested a Krillion-inspired question-and-prize game themed around Meet Fresh and mango sago, with points for niche answers and speed.

**How to apply:** Keep answer classification server-side and distinct from the illustrative homepage story. Use server timing for scoring, never browser-supplied elapsed times.

Each round is 25 seconds, including practice.

**Why:** The user explicitly said "it should be 25 seconds each."

**How to apply:** Keep multiplayer deadlines, practice resets, countdowns, and speed-bonus timing consistent with 25 seconds. Do not offer longer round-length choices.

Speed points must vary with how fast the player answers, with a 20-point maximum, not a fixed 20-point award. The maximum does not scale with answer rarity.

**Why:** The user clarified that "the speed points is variable, not set to 20," then confirmed "20 point maximum, just based on the timing of things."

**How to apply:** Show the actual calculated bonus in results and describe it as time-dependent; do not imply everyone receives 20 points.

There is no shared score. Each player submits one answer per round; show the top three after every round and a filterable catalog of all possible answers after time expires.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the initial cooperative interpretation: "There is no shared score." They selected one answer per round.

**How to apply:** Preserve individual cumulative standings and round podiums. Do not add shared/team scoring or permit repeated attempts. Do not reveal the answer catalog before expiry.

Treat spreadsheet "Too Clever" entries as lowest-tier answers. Guests customize their Bobi in the lobby.

**Why:** The user selected lowest-tier points for "Too Clever" and requested distinct player appearances when joining.

**How to apply:** Preserve the same recognizable uploaded character while providing saved appearance choices.

The host's lobby should show joining players' customized Bobis together in a shared milk-tea scene.

**Why:** The user asked for each player's Bobi to appear on the server screen as they join, suggesting a milk-tea setting.

**How to apply:** Use the actual live room roster and saved appearances; do not populate the shared cup with pretend players.

Players answer on their phones, then see a private Bobi dive to the reward for their answer and an updated running total at the top left. The host screen shows the question and who has answered, not private answer content or a player's dive.

**Why:** After earlier removing all gameplay dives, the user explicitly reintroduced them on the playing screen: after writing an answer "they will dive like in Krillion" and see their total points. This supersedes the earlier no-diving rule for phones only.

**How to apply:** Reveal only the submitting player's own reward and total immediately. Keep other answers, current-round standings, and the catalog hidden until time expires. At timeout the host shows the top-three Bobi podium and filterable potential answers, with host-only Next. Preserve phone results as well. Do not restore the homepage intro.

The question screen should fill the viewport with a mango-sago game scene, with a floating question and a countdown beside the bottom answer input. Start at the surface; deeper ingredients and tiers should be unknown until the dive reaches them. Practice should feel like a real question round, not a fitting room.

**Why:** The user rejected the small drink picture and later the exposed cross-section, supplied a full-screen Krillion gameplay reference, and explicitly said "We shouldnt KNOW the tiers already."

**How to apply:** Keep customization in joining/lobby, not practice. Reuse the full-screen player layout for practice, clearly distinguished from real server-scored play. Do not show tier ladders, ordered rewards or tier names in pre-round instructions. Only reveal the earned reward after landing; the full catalog still opens after time expires.

Do not narrate "Bobi found a…" after the dive.

**Why:** The user explicitly said there was no need for "bobi found a pomelo gem."

**How to apply:** Use the earned reward and points display without a redundant discovery sentence.

Practice should allow unlimited time to inspect the screen before starting a 25-second attempt; start its clock with the first nonblank edit and provide visible retry/next controls after submission or expiry.

**Why:** Practice visitors may be reviewing the artwork before typing. Expiring the input while they inspect the scene prevents them from trying the game. The user repeatedly reported that practice would not let them type and specifically requested game sound during practice.

**How to apply:** This is a practice-only timing choice. Real multiplayer rounds must retain their shared server-started deadline and speed scoring, unaffected by when any guest first types. Do not leave expired practice inputs disabled: typing can begin a fresh attempt. The phone question preview should also be playable practice with game music, rather than a separate silent, paused experience.

Do not show a "Replay dive" action on the per-question reward.

**Why:** The user explicitly said there was no need for it.

**How to apply:** Play the earned dive once and leave its result visible. Keep practice's fresh-attempt and next-question controls separate from animation replay.

Forgive small answer typos only when there is one clear prepared-answer match.

**Why:** The user chose "Forgive small answer typos" over typing suggestions, after being offered conservative matching that would not change an unusual answer into something else.

**How to apply:** Prefer exact answers; do not guess between plausible answers or favor higher rarity. Preserve what each player typed, award the matched answer's normal rarity and server-timed speed points, and keep previously saved scores unchanged.
